# Result-level relationship to the companion technical LEAP study

Paper 2 is sole-authored by Meshaal Obaid Mouawad and does not depend on acceptance of the companion technical manuscript.

## Evidence already present in the companion technical study

The companion technical study establishes the LEAP source-to-record architecture and reports the underlying frozen software artifact, including:

- the Ethylene Production Yield source and its non-positive input guard;
- the Gross Margin declared/executable denominator disagreement and visible `Needs Review` routing;
- the existence and generation of the 28-dossier sample-project build; and
- the underlying publication/generator implementation used to create the dossier pages.

These facts are reused as software-artifact evidence and are not reclaimed as new Paper 2 findings.

## Results added by Paper 2

Paper 2 contributes a distinct publication-audit question and reports:

- operationalization of four literature-grounded publication obligations with explicit applicability rules;
- complete-corpus classification of source-role, guard-disclosure, disagreement-routing, and authority-language outcomes;
- reduction of repeated manifestations to three generator defect classes plus one working routing mechanism;
- source-level root-cause localization for those defect classes;
- the formal positive-domain lower-bound analysis of the Gross Margin disagreement;
- within-LEAP persistence and input-sensitivity checks;
- checker-validation controls, including negative and benign controls on actual archived artifacts; and
- the external FastAPI/OpenAPI transferability pilot.

The Paper 2 supplement contains the evidence and scripts required to reproduce those Paper 2 outcomes without relying on acceptance of the companion manuscript.
