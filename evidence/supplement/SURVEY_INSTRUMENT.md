# Recovered survey instrument and analyzed items

Paper 2 analyzes one survey sample of **N = 60 participants**. The three items used in the main Paper 2 results are Q04, Q06, and Q07. Each analyzed category distribution totals 60 responses. Percentages use this denominator, with no imputation or removal of category counts in the present analysis.

## Documentary basis

This document reproduces question wording and response fields retained in the research archive. It is a recovered instrument record, rather than a dated original questionnaire for the full sample. The N=60 profile and analyzed distributions are provided in the adjacent CSV files.

The retained study metadata describes purposive/convenience sampling across enterprise-system roles. The documented basis is summarized in the archived Study 2 account, `LEAP_Study2_v8_N60.pdf`, Section 4.4.1 and Appendix C.6. The supplied records do not specify the recruitment channel, organizational sampling frame, original invitation procedure, formal eligibility criteria, or invitation denominator. Dated entries span 10–30 August 2025; the complete collection period is unavailable.
